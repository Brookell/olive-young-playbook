Course brief

What the professor expects.

What the professor asks

  1. Who are you, and who is your team?
  2. What makes it unique, with its own identity?
  3. What are the cultural differences in your team?
  4. How do you make these differences opportunities?
  5. How do you make your multicultural team efficient?

Must include: Golden Circle, Vision Tree, Blazon, Culture Map, and a first 12-month action plan with concrete examples.

His feedback on other groups

  • Everyone speaks and engages the audience
  • Be creative; don’t read from phones or slides
  • One consistent slide design
  • A detailed, specific action plan
  • Explain the target market and the unique product
  • Say exactly which AI tools we used, for what, and how
